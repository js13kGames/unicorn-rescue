**The most challenging unicorn shmup 🦄💥**

Free the unicorns from steam-powered goblins in **Unicorn Rescue**, a fast pastel shooter. Break cages, dodge bullet storms and take down the flying fortress. Get close for bigger scores and graze bullets to charge your power: **Horn Blade** at half charge, **Rainbow Blast** at full charge!

**43 unicorns. One fighter. A sky full of trouble.**


## Controls

| Action | Desktop | Mobile |
|---|---|---|
| Start / advance through the briefing | `Space` | Tap the screen |
| Move | `↑` `↓` `←` `→`, `WASD` or `ZQSD` | Drag a finger |
| Move precisely | Hold `Shift` | Small finger movements |
| Fire | Automatic | Automatic |
| Trigger the charged power | `Space` | Bottom-right button or two fingers |
| Pause | `P` or `Esc` | `II` button |
| Resume | `P`, `Esc` or `Space` | Tap the screen |
| Mute / unmute | `M` | — |

### Two powers, a single button

**Horn Blade**, available at half gauge, replaces your projectiles with a continuous beam that pierces through enemies. **Rainbow Blast**, at full gauge, adds two side beams and a five-way shot; enemy bullets caught in its beams turn into stars. Trigger them with `Space` or the touch button: the amount of charge decides which power you get.

### Mini-bosses and the flying fortress

Armored vehicles harden the crossing until the airship arrives, raining projectiles
and reinforcements. Beat it to win. Rescuing **all 43 unicorns** is the true goal;
the best score is saved locally.

**Playable with 10 achievements and 3 leaderboards on [Wavedash](https://wavedash.com/games/unicorn-rescue).**
